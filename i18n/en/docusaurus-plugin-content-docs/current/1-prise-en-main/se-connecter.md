## Cloud edition

### Create your instance

<https://app.flowanalyticspro.com/sign-up>

On the login page, click Sign up and follow the steps. The person who creates the instance becomes its owner: they then manage users and the subscription. A new instance comes with a 30-day free trial.

### Log in

Two login methods are available:

- **with your email and password**;
- **with your Google, Microsoft or Atlassian account**, using OAuth2 authentication. You then have no FAP password to remember.

If you forgot your password, click Lost password? on the login page.

### Multi-instance

A user can belong to several FAP instances, with the same user account.

Once authenticated, a user who belongs to several FAP instances can choose which instance to access.

## On-Premise edition

Accounts are created by an instance administrator, in user management. You receive an email to confirm your account.

Depending on how your organization has configured FAP, you can log in:

- **with your email and password**;
- **with your Google, Microsoft or Atlassian account**, using OAuth2 authentication;
- **with your company’s single sign-on (SSO)**, using the SAML2 protocol. You then use the same credentials as for your other company tools.

The first time you log in with OAuth2 or SSO, your FAP account is created automatically with the User role. An administrator must then give you access to the products you need.

## Jira add-on

There is no separate FAP login. Open FAP from the Jira apps menu: you access it through your Jira account.

## First login

In the Cloud and On-Premise editions, the first time you log in, FAP asks you to complete your profile and accept the general terms of use and subscription.
