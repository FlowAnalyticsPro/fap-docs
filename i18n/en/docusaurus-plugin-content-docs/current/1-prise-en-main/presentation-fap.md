## What Flow Analytics Pro is for

Flow Analytics Pro (FAP) helps teams and their managers understand and improve their value stream. The app collects data from the tools your teams already use, such as Jira, GitHub or GitLab, and turns it into metrics, charts and analyses. There is nothing to re-enter.

With FAP, you can:

- see how long it takes to complete an issue, and how many issues a team completes each week;
- spot the stages that slow work down and the issues that are aging;
- forecast a completion date or a delivery volume, with a confidence level;
- track how often and how reliably you deploy to production;
- assess team maturity and track progress.

## The modules

FAP is organized into four modules. Each one has its own color in the app.

- **Flow Metrics** (blue): metrics on your teams’ workflow, calculated from Jira or an Excel file. Flow time, throughput, work in progress, efficiency, forecasts.
- **DORA Metrics** (green): the four software delivery performance metrics, calculated from GitHub or GitLab. Deployment frequency, change lead time, change fail rate and mean time to recovery.
- **Assessments** (yellow): assessment templates to measure team maturity and track progress over time.
- **Alice**: the analysis assistant built into FAP. She analyzes a chart, a sprint or an entire product and offers findings and recommendations.

## The three FAP editions

FAP comes in three editions, and this guide covers all of them. When a screen or a feature applies to only some of them, the section says so at the top.

- **Cloud**: FAP runs in your browser, with nothing to install. You create your instance by signing up, and you manage your subscription from the app.
- **On-Premise**: FAP is installed on your organization’s servers. It works the same way as the Cloud edition, without subscription management, and adds SAML2 single sign-on.
- **Jira add-on**: FAP is installed in Jira Cloud from the Atlassian Marketplace and opens directly in Jira. You access it through your Jira account: there is no sign-up, and no user or role management in FAP. The license is managed in Jira.
