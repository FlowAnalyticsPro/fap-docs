## The side menu

The side menu, on the left of the screen, gives access to all the modules. Hover over an icon to display its name. The menu is organized in groups:

- **Home**.
- **Flow Metrics**: Products and Programs.
- **DORA Metrics**: Views and Programs.
- **Assessments**: Templates, Teams and Programs.
- **Administration**: Users administration, in the Cloud and On-Premise editions, and Settings, which gather the parameters shared by the whole instance. These entries are reserved for administrators.

At the bottom of the menu, you find:

- **Help**: access to the user guide and to the version history;
- **the language switch**, between French and English;
- **Account**: your profile and logging out.

In the Jira module, the language button and the Account menu do not appear: FAP is displayed in the language of your Jira profile, French or English. To change the language, change it in your Jira profile.

## Home

Home is the first page displayed after you log in. It shows:

- **the module overview**, with the number of items in each module;
- **news and updates**: the release notes, the demo videos and the latest blog articles;
- **documentation and resources**, with a guide for each module and an introduction to Alice.

## Portfolios

Each menu entry first opens a portfolio: the list of the module’s items, for example all your products or all your DORA views. From the portfolio, you can open an item, create or import a new one, or delete one.

The products portfolio is displayed as cards or as a list. The button at the top right, List or Cards, switches from one display to the other.

## An item’s dashboard

When you open a product, a program, a DORA view or an assessment, you land on its dashboard. All dashboards are built the same way.

- **The header** shows the item’s name, its module and the date of the last data update.
- **The header buttons** give access to the item’s different views, for example Dashboard, Charts, Issues List or Settings. They also lead to the linked items.
- **The main area** displays the selected view.
