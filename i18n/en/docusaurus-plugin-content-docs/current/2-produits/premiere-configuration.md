## What this step is for

Before computing the metrics of a new product, FAP needs to know which issues to count and how to read your workflow. That is the purpose of the initial configuration. It is mandatory.

## How to get there

Products portfolio › product card › Review configuration. The product settings open automatically on the two mandatory settings.

## What you can do

1. **Issue Types**: check the issue types to use for KPI calculation, and those that represent bugs. At least one type must be used. Click Apply.
2. **Workflow**: place each step of your tool in one of FAP’s three categories, To Do, WIP or Done, and indicate for each WIP step whether it is active work or waiting. Click Apply.
3. If needed, check the other settings, in particular the working days and the KPI calculation period.
4. Go back to the portfolio and click Start computation. The product is ready when the progress bar disappears.

The details of these settings are in section 2.9.

## Good to know

- Only administrators can do the initial configuration. In the Jira module, any user can.
- Take the time to set up the workflow carefully: all flow times depend on it.
